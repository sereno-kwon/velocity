Brand

VelocityGrand Rapids, MI · Since 1989

Started in a backyard shed,
now built one wheel at a time
in a Michigan factory.

Velocity was born in 1989 in a backyard shed outside Brisbane, Australia. Founder Tom Black made aluminum water bottle cages by hand on his own, and his brother John Black took them to the United States. In 1991 Velocity introduced its first aluminum rim, the Aero, and became a rim brand.

In 1992 Velocity opened a US distribution center in Grand Rapids, Michigan, and in 2012 it moved its entire Australian production to Jacksonville, Florida. Since bringing all production to Grand Rapids in 2016, Velocity remains one of the few brands making aluminum rims in the United States.

Craft

Roll, drill, anodize, build.
From extrusion to wheel, under one roof.

At the Velocity factory in Grand Rapids, aluminum extrusions are rolled into hoops, drilled for spokes and anodized for colour, then laced to hubs and spokes in the Wheel Department to become finished wheels. Good, locally sourced materials, and every step within arm's reach.

That is why choosing a Velocity rim is fun. Each model comes in different hole counts, sizes, brake types and a range of anodized colours. Finding the right combination for your bike and your riding is where every Velocity wheel begins.

Made in USARims made in Michigan

At a time when importing rims is the norm, Velocity makes its aluminum rims in its own American factory.

HandbuiltWheels laced by hand

Builders in the Wheel Department set spoke tension, true and dish one wheel at a time. Wheels that last come from human hands.

ColourAnodized colours

Not just black and silver. Velocity's colour rims have become the easiest way to give a bike its own character.

02

Classic · Track

Deep V · ChukkerDeep V · Chukker

The rim city fixed gear
noticed first.
Deep, stiff and built to last.

The Deep V is Velocity's signature rim, with a 30mm-tall V-shaped profile. The stiffness of that deep section makes for strong wheels even with fewer spokes, and it stands up to the track, fixed gear and rough city streets for years. It comes with a machined brake track (MSW) or without (non-MSW), and in a range of anodized colours.

The Chukker is the Deep V ATB, reissued. Its deep profile is made even tougher to stand up to heavier riders, tandems and the constant knocks of bike polo.

03

Road · Tubeless Ready

Quill · AileronQuill · Aileron

Rim brake and disc,
two answers for the road.
Wider, and tubeless.

The Quill follows the long-loved A23 road rim. Using design borrowed from the Aileron, it is wider and stiffer than the A23, with a 25mm-tall profile and a tubeless-ready bed. It is the Velocity that suits rim-brake road bikes and classic bicycles best.

The Aileron is a disc-only all-round road rim. Its 28mm-tall, 25mm-wide profile supports wide road tyres securely, and tubeless setup is simple. Road riding and light gravel on one wheelset.

Touring · Commute

Cliffhanger · AtlasCliffhanger · Atlas

Loaded up,
ridden every day,
and still worry-free.

The Cliffhanger is a tough rim for touring and commuting. Its wide profile supports big tyres securely, and it can run tubeless for fewer flats. If you want a long-lasting wheel for the bike you ride every day, start with the Cliffhanger.

The Atlas, as the name suggests, is a rim that carries the weight. It is made for long-distance touring with fully loaded panniers and comes in 700C and 650B. Built with 36 holes, it handles heavy loads and rough roads together.

05

Gravel · XC · Adventure

Blunt SS · Blunt 35 · DuallyBlunt · Dually

Rims that get wider
where the road ends.
Gravel, mountains and beyond.

Blunt is Velocity's off-road rim family. The third-generation Blunt SS is a light, disc-only rim, 30mm wide outside and 26.6mm inside, made for XC and gravel. The Blunt 35 is a trail rim with a 30mm inner width that holds bigger MTB tyres firmly.

The Dually is a 45mm-wide rim. The wide bed spreads the tyre out for a bigger contact patch and keeps it from folding at low pressure. A rim for loaded bikepacking and adventure bikes crossing sand and gravel.

Story

1989 ~ 2026Brisbane · Grand Rapids

From a shed in Brisbane
to a factory in Grand Rapids,
the Velocity story.

From a shed in 1989 where water bottle cages were made by hand, to today's factory making aluminum rims in the United States. Velocity moved its production across an ocean, but never let go of the principle of making its own rims and wheels.
